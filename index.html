    LLM Providers Table

    What is this for?

    Here you will find free, freemium, and paid providers, that provide access to large language models. It includes limits, registration requirements, the ability to quickly copy the Base URL, and direct links for registration/getting an API key.

    Help tablet! Share it!

    The more people who use it, the more motivated I'll be to update it, add new providers, and keep everything up to date. Post a link to the table in chat, in repo, in comments, or on Discord server - anywhere 🙏

    How to use the table

    Click or tap the badges and requirement icons to see short explanations.

    Main fields

    • Base URL: A ready-to-use address for connecting to the API in your code or application.
    • Models: A list of key large language models available in the service. The globe icon usually leads to the full list. (You can also look at the list of all models from the provider, but the prices there do not reflect the provider's pricing and model capabilities; the information there is from official providers)
    • Limits: API usage restrictions. RPM - requests per minute, RPH - per hour, RPD - per day, etc.
    • Provider badges: Service status and testing status are shown next to the provider name.

    Icon legend

    • Navigation:

    • Globe icon - clickable link to the corresponding page (registration, getting a key, viewing models).

    • Provider badges:

    • Warning - the provider has concerns listed on the Warning page.
    • Official - the provider hosts models themselves (official service).
    • Official Partner - an official partner or reseller of a specific provider.
    • Aggregator / AI Gateway - routes requests to other providers' APIs (e.g., OpenRouter).
    • In Development - the service is still in development.
    • Unofficial - unofficial or community-driven service.
    • Unknown - the nature of the service is unknown.
    • Deprecated - the service is no longer active.
    • Community - community-driven open-source project.
    • Mirror - a mirror of another provider's API endpoint.
    • Experimental - the service is experimental and may be unstable.
    • Tested - tested by the author.
    • Untested - not tested yet.

    • In Progress - currently being tested.

    • Registration requirements:

    • Registration icon - standard registration on the website.
    • Phone icon - phone number verification is required.
    • Card icon - a bank card must be linked.
    • Special icon - special registration conditions, filled in manually for that provider.
    • Discord icon - Discord account required.

    Categories

    • Freemium providers - providers that are not completely free, but provide credits/money to your account upon registration.
    • Free providers - providers that offer access to models absolutely for free.
    • Paid - services that require payment or operate on a credit-based system.
    • Warning - providers with unconfirmed concerns, suspicious behavior, or trust issues that don't rise to "dangerous" level.
    • Dangerous / Not Recommended - providers with serious community concerns or trust issues. (It's empty for now)
